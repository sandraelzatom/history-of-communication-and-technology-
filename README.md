# Museum Project: The Distance Defeaters
## A Narrative History of Communication Technology

### 1. Executive Summary
This digital exhibit traces the evolution of communication technology, focusing on the transition from mechanical signals to digital data. By exploring the "tools of connection," the museum highlights how engineering has consistently overcome the physical limitations of distance.

### 2. Identity Framework
* **Visual Tradition:** Bauhaus. I chose this style to reflect the intersection of art and industry, emphasizing geometric layouts that mirror the structured nature of communication networks.
* **Brand Archetype:** The Sage. The museum acts as a guardian of historical milestones, providing a thoughtful, technical, and visionary perspective on human progress.
* **Persuasion Method (Cialdini):** Authority. By utilizing primary source documents (patents, blueprints) and a structured archival layout, the site establishes credibility and encourages deep educational engagement.

### 3. Agentic Orchestration (Process)
I used a spec-driven AI workflow to ensure high-quality output:
1.  **Phase 1: Spec Design:** I directed the AI to review the "NextJS AI Orchestration" reference repo to establish a Sprint/QA workflow.
2.  **Phase 2: Smithsonian Curator Persona:** I prompted the AI to act as a Senior Curator to review my artifact selection. It suggested narrowing the focus from "all tech" to "distance-breaking tech."
3.  **Phase 3: Development Sprints:**
    * **Sprint A:** Layout & Bauhaus Grid system.
    * **Sprint B:** Interactive Timeline & Framer Motion animations.
    * **Sprint C:** Artifact integration and responsive testing.
4.  **Phase 4: Final QA:** A "Design Auditor" agent reviewed the site for color contrast and accessibility to ensure it met professional museum standards.

### 4. Interactive Features
* **Animated Signal Flow:** A visual representation of data moving through wires/air.
* **Digital Archive:** High-resolution scans of original patents and technical diagrams.
* **Dynamic Typography:** Text that "types out" in Morse code before resolving into English.
